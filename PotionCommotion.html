PROJECT SUMMARY


Potion Commotion is a game about brewing unique potions to sell to customers and escape debt by completing micro-minigames. This was done for my 9-week university project where 27 students (including myself) collaboratively developed a game.

I decided to put myself forward as the producer for this project to step out of my comfort zone, this put great emphasis on my communication and organisational skills. Furthermore, due to our teams composition, I also assisted with a large amount of design work including documentation; level design, affordance and brainstormnig in design meetings.
