PROJECT SUMMARY


CHVMBER is the prototype for a single player roguelike where rather than upgrading the player, you upgrade the bullets. This was a industry project where I worked with 5 others of different disciplines to quickly develop a vertical slice. It has then been given to a larger team within the studio to develop further (the version on this website is before this).

The initial concept for this project was my own which I pitched to the studio in competition with other projects. As it was picked, I became the design lead and developed a comprehensive GDD and documentation to support the others in the studio. I led many group meetings with the line managers to keep them up to date propose new concepts. I also spent a lot of time in engine supporting the other programmers with UI for the HUD.
