SENTINEL

DESIGNER / PROGRAMMER

PROJECT SUMMARY


Sentinel is a small "thriller" game where the player must navigate an abandoned station while trying to stay out of the darkness! This project was developed using a Framework called Sentinel, hence the name.

For this project, I altered many of the framework mechanics to include new mechanics surrounding it. I also created the cutscenes, story, objectives and level design for the game. I am very proud of it, especially for my entry into UE5!
