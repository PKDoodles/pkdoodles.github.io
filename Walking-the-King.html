PROJECT SUMMARY


Walking the King is a small platformer where you must balance an egg on your head. This was an industry project where I worked with 5 others of different disciplines to quickly develop a vertical slice. This was an onboarded project handed to us by a separate team, we were tasked with recoding the game and adding minor mechanics to the enivronment.

As Design Lead in a very small team, I had a wealth of tasks such as: designing and implementing mechanics such as traps and player feel; level design and meshing; and leading design meetings. Furthermore I developed new skills such as the Unreal Engine landscaping tools to help the artists!
