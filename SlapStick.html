SLAP STICK

DESIGNER / PROGRAMMER GDD

PROJECT SUMMARY


Slap Stick is a small FPS game where you cannot kill enemies, you instead must shoot objects to make them fall to the floor. This game mixes comedy and action to make a small enjoyable experience! This project was developed using a Framework called Lancer.

For this project, I altered many of the framework mechanics to include a new weapon and the mechanics surrounding it. I also created the cutscenes, story, objectives and level design for the game. I am incredibly proud of how it works!
