PROJECT SUMMARY


Mainspring is a concept for a larger scale isometric game with a focus on altering your pocket watch to cast different spells. For this porject, I focused on making a super coesive desgin document that covers as many basis as possible, clarity of words and visual tools where my top priority!

I also considered other aspects such as pricing methods, marketing and scope management. Which has given me a better understanding of the games industry as a whole and how to make a game that is balances scope and profitability! It was also nice to not have to worry about actually making the game and instead focus on the design and planning aspects of game development.
